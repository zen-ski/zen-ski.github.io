Privacy Policy – AMC MCQ Exam Prep

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile application "AMC MCQ Exam Prep" (the "App"), published by Zenski ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use it on Android or iOS devices. The App package and bundle identifier is com.zenski.amcprep.

Educational Disclaimer: AMC MCQ Exam Prep is an independent educational training tool for International Medical Graduates (IMGs) and candidates preparing for the AMC CAT MCQ Examination. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Australian Medical Council (AMC), the Medical Board of Australia, or AHPRA.

1. Summary & Core Principles

  • No Account Required: You are not required to create an account, log in, or provide personal details such as your name, email address, or phone number to use the App.
  • Local Device Storage: Your study progress, mock exam scores, subtopic completion records, answer persistence, and bookmarks are saved solely on your local device via local storage.
  • Advertising: The free tier of the App displays ads powered by Google AdMob. Google may process pseudonymous device identifiers and diagnostic data to deliver and measure advertisements, as detailed below.
  • In-App Purchases: Premium upgrades ("AMC Master PRO") are processed directly by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive, process, or store your payment card or financial details.

2. Information Collected & Stored Locally

All core learning data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Saved answers across clinical subtopics (Adult Health, Surgery, Women’s Health, Paediatrics, Mental Health, Population Health & Ethics).
  • Mock exam history, scaled 0–500 score benchmark records, and pass/fail probability diagnostics.
  • Bookmarked questions and personalized Mistakes Hub revision questions.
  • In-app settings, theme preferences, and subscription entitlement status.

3. Third-Party Services & SDKs

The App integrates the following third-party SDKs:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads on the free tier. Google AdMob may collect pseudonymous identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / IDFA), coarse device location, and diagnostic interaction events. You can opt out of personalized ads via your device's operating system settings. For details, visit Google Advertising Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play In-App Billing / Apple StoreKit: Used to process purchases of the AMC Master PRO upgrade. Purchase tokens and entitlements are verified through the store platforms.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all study history is stored locally on your device:

  • You can clear all study progress and subtopic answers at any time using the in-app "Restart Subtopic" or data reset options.
  • Uninstalling the application from your device permanently deletes all locally stored questions progress, mock results, and cached data.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for medical graduates and healthcare professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

6. Security

Any network communication made by integrated SDKs (e.g., Google AdMob and Google Play Billing) is transmitted using secure Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS encryption).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
