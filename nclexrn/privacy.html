Privacy Policy – NCLEX RN Prep: NextGen

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile application "NCLEX RN Prep: NextGen" (the "App"), published by Zenski ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use it on Android or iOS devices. The App package and bundle identifier is com.zenski.nclexrn.

Educational & Trademark Disclaimer: NCLEX®, NCLEX-RN®, and NGN® are registered trademarks of the National Council of State Boards of Nursing, Inc. (NCSBN®). This App is an independent educational training aid and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or associated with NCSBN. Practice test scores do not predict or guarantee an official NCLEX-RN passing result.

1. Summary & Core Principles

  • No Account Required: You are not required to create an account, register, or provide personal details such as your legal name, email address, or telephone number to use the App.
  • Local Device Storage: Your entire learning trajectory—including quiz answers, 2,254-question completion records, CJMM clinical judgment scores, mock exam history, bookmarked questions, and customized notes—is stored strictly on your local device.
  • Advertising: The free tier of the App displays ads served by Google AdMob. Google may process pseudonymous device identifiers and diagnostic signals to deliver and measure advertisements, as detailed below.
  • In-App Purchases: Premium upgrades ("NCLEX RN Pro") are processed directly through Google Play Billing or Apple StoreKit. We do not receive, process, or store your credit card, banking, or billing credentials.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores learning data locally on your device using secure local storage (SharedPreferences on Android and NSUserDefaults on iOS):

  • Question responses and answer states across all eight 2026 NCLEX Client Needs categories;
  • Clinical Judgment Measurement Model (CJMM) radar metrics (Recognize Cues, Analyze Cues, Prioritize Hypotheses, Generate Solutions, Take Action, Evaluate Outcomes);
  • Full-length CAT mock exam logs, scaled test scores, and performance analytics;
  • Saved questions, bookmarked items, and personalized Missed Questions remediation banks;
  • App user preferences (theme selection, Pearson VUE exam mode toggle, sound settings);
  • NCLEX Pro entitlement and purchase receipt verification tokens.

This data never leaves your device, is never transmitted to remote servers operated by Zenski, and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear app storage in your device settings.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. To deliver compliant ads, combat fraudulent activity, and report aggregated performance metrics, Google may collect and process:

  • Device or advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID or Apple Identifier for Advertisers [IDFA]);
  • Coarse/approximate geographic location inferred from IP address;
  • Ad engagement events (impressions, clicks, completed rewarded video views);
  • SDK diagnostic telemetry, app crash logs, and performance metrics;
  • Device hardware profile (manufacturer, model, OS version, locale, and screen resolution).

Google acts as an independent data controller for advertising services. For full details on Google's privacy practices, see:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

4. User Consent, Choices & Opt-Outs

  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: Before personalized ads are served, the App presents a consent dialog powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), an IAB TCF v2.2 certified Consent Management Platform (CMP). You can review or modify your consent preferences at any time in the in-app Settings.
  • Apple iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS, the App requests permission before accessing the IDFA. If you choose "Ask App not to Track", your IDFA will not be used for advertising tracking.
  • Android Advertising Settings: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time via Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Ad-Free Experience: You can permanently remove all advertisements by unlocking NCLEX RN Pro within the App.

5. In-App Purchases & Billing

Transactions for NCLEX RN Pro are handled exclusively by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Google and Apple confirm purchases by delivering cryptographic receipt tokens to the App. We never have access to your payment card details, bank information, or home billing address.

6. Data We Do NOT Collect

We practice strict data minimization. We do NOT:

  • Collect your name, email address, physical address, or phone number;
  • Access your camera, microphone, photo library, or contacts;
  • Collect or monitor precise GPS location;
  • Sell, rent, monetize, or trade any personal information to data brokers;
  • Run background tracking services to trace individual user activities outside the App.

7. Regulatory Compliance

We comply with applicable privacy regulations, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). Because your learning records reside on your local physical device, you maintain full sovereignty and custody over your personal data at all times.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is an educational test preparation resource tailored for nursing students, nursing graduates, and practicing healthcare professionals. The App is not directed toward children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Data Retention & Erasure

All test scores and study logs remain on your device until you manually clear your history inside the App or uninstall the application. Deleting the App immediately purges all locally stored records.

10. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app improvements, new features, or statutory updates. Any revisions will be published at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
