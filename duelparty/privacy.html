2 Player Games: Duel Party

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game "2 Player Games: Duel Party" (the "App"), published by Zenski ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it on Android or iOS. The App identifier is com.zenski.duelparty.

1. Summary

  • We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts.
  • Player names you type, your settings, match records, statistics and progress are stored only on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • If the App crashes, an anonymous crash report is sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the problem. The App also downloads game settings (such as how often ads appear) from Google Firebase Remote Config, and uses Google Analytics for Firebase only to count how many devices use the App.
  • The optional "Premium" purchase (no ads, every theme and unlimited hints) is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following locally on your phone or tablet: settings (sound, vibration, layout and color-blind palette), player profiles you create (a name, a color and an avatar icon), head-to-head match records between those players, records against the computer players, Game Night cup progress, favorites, statistics, daily-duel results and streaks, experience points, ranks, achievements and whether you bought "Premium". Player names can be anything you choose; they are optional. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show full-screen ads between matches (never during play). To serve, personalise (where permitted), measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect and process:

  • Device or other identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID or, on iOS, the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) if you allow tracking;
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • App interactions, such as ad views and taps;
  • Diagnostics and crash information related to the ads SDK;
  • Device information such as model, operating system version and language.

This data is collected and processed by Google, which acts as an independent controller or processor under its own policies. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services — https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and the Google Privacy Policy — https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Consent and your choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: before personalised ads are shown, the App asks for your consent using Google's User Messaging Platform (a certified consent management platform). You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Privacy settings.
  • iOS: the App asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised using it. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • You can stop all ads by buying "Premium" in the App, and you can stop all data collection by uninstalling the App.

5. In-app purchases

The optional "Premium" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing or Apple In-App Purchase. It shares only the purchase status with the App so it can remove ads, unlock every theme and restore your purchase. We do not receive or store your payment card or billing details. Purchase data is processed under Google's or Apple's privacy policy.

6. Crash reports and app settings (Google Firebase)

The App uses three Google Firebase services. None of them is used for advertising, and none receives your player names or match records.

  • Firebase Crashlytics: when the App crashes or hits a serious error, it sends a crash report: the error and the code location (stack trace), which game was open, the App version, device model, operating system version, language, free memory and disk space, and a random Firebase installation identifier. We use these reports only to find and fix bugs. Learn more: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: used only to count active devices (for example, how many people are playing right now). It records that the App was opened and how long a session lasted, with the App version, device model, operating system, language and approximate country (from the IP address), linked to a random app-instance identifier. The App does not send any custom events, does not collect the advertising identifier for this, and does not allow this data to be used for ads personalisation.
  • Firebase Remote Config: the App downloads a few settings from Google, such as how many matches pass between ads. This request includes the random Firebase installation identifier and basic device and App information.

Crash reports are kept by Firebase for up to 90 days and Analytics data for up to 2 months. The Firebase installation identifier is not linked to your identity and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

7. What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not run our own servers, we do not use analytics to track you or for advertising, and we do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data.

8. Children

The App is a general-audience game collection that families can play together, but it is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays until you delete the App or its data. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies; you can reset your advertising identifier at any time. Crash reports are deleted by Firebase after 90 days. Because we do not hold personal data on our own servers, there is nothing else for us to delete, but you may contact us with any request.

10. Security

Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and by Firebase is encrypted in transit.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because the App does not collect personal data on our own servers, most requests are handled through the choices in section 4 or directly with Google. You can also contact us at any time.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be reflected in an App update.
