Australian Pharmacy Council (APC) Exam Prep

Privacy Policy – OPRA Exam Prep

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile application "OPRA Exam Prep" (the "App"), published by Zenski ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use it on Android or iOS devices. The App package and bundle identifier is com.zenski.opra.

1. Summary & Core Principles

  • No Account Required: You are not required to create an account, log in, or provide personal details such as your name, email address, or phone number to use the App.
  • Local Device Storage: Your study progress, mock exam scores, domain readiness statistics, bookmarked questions, and mistake bank history are saved solely on your local device.
  • Advertising: The free tier of the App displays ads powered by Google AdMob. Google may process device identifiers and diagnostic data to deliver and measure advertisements, as detailed below.
  • In-App Purchases: Premium upgrades ("OPRA Pro") are processed directly by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive, process, or store your payment card or financial details.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your smartphone or tablet using secure device storage (SharedPreferences / NSUserDefaults):

  • Exam sessions, answers submitted, completion timestamps, and percentage scores;
  • Performance breakdowns across the 5 APC syllabus domains (Therapeutics, Biomedical Sciences, Pharmacology & Toxicology, Pharmacy Practice & Legislation, Calculation & QUM);
  • Bookmarked questions and mistake bank question logs;
  • App preferences (theme selection, completed onboarding status, remaining free mock attempts);
  • OPRA Pro subscription / lifetime purchase receipt verification tokens.

This data never leaves your device, is never transmitted to any external server owned or operated by Zenski, and is permanently deleted when you uninstall the App or clear app data.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display banner, interstitial, and app open advertisements. To deliver relevant ads, prevent ad fraud, and provide analytics, Google may automatically collect and process certain device information:

  • Device or advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID or Apple Identifier for Advertisers - IDFA, collected strictly in accordance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework);
  • Coarse/approximate location derived from IP address;
  • Interaction data, such as ad views, impressions, and click events;
  • Diagnostic and crash logs related to the advertising SDK;
  • Hardware specifications, including device model, operating system version, and system language.

Google handles this data as an independent controller under its own privacy terms. For detailed information, please review:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

4. User Consent, Choices & Opt-Outs

  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: Before personalized ads are served, the App requests your explicit consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a certified Consent Management Platform (CMP). You can review or modify your consent settings at any time within the App.
  • Apple iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS devices, the App asks for permission before accessing the IDFA. If you choose "Ask App not to Track", your IDFA is not used for ad personalization.
  • Android Ad Personalization: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time via your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Ad-Free Experience: You can permanently remove all advertisements by upgrading to OPRA Pro within the App.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Payments for "OPRA Pro" (Monthly Subscription or Lifetime Access) are handled exclusively by Apple App Store (In-App Purchase) or Google Play Billing. Apple and Google provide us only with cryptographic purchase status tokens to enable premium features and remove ads. We never have access to your credit card number, bank details, or billing address.

6. Data We Do NOT Collect

We believe in data minimization. We do NOT:

  • Collect your real name, email, phone number, or contacts;
  • Access your photos, files, camera, or microphone;
  • Track your precise GPS location;
  • Sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party;
  • Operate tracking servers to monitor individual user behavior.

7. Australian Privacy Principles (APP) & International Compliance

We comply with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Because user data is stored solely on your device and not on remote servers, you maintain direct, physical control over your data at all times.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is an educational examination preparation tool designed for pharmacy graduates and adult healthcare professionals. It is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

All study data remains on your device until you delete individual items in the App (such as clearing your mistake bank) or uninstall the application. You can immediately erase all locally stored data by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in device settings.

10. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or legal changes. Any updates will be published at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
