PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile app "Kindlight: Meditation & Sleep" (the "App"), published by Zenski ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it on Android or iOS. The App package / bundle ID is com.zenski.kindlight.

1. Summary

  • There is no account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Everything you write or record in the App (journal entries, check-ins, moods, habits, notes, affirmations, plans) is stored only on your device, encrypted. It is never sent to us.
  • We do not show ads, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell any information.
  • Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • The App downloads guidance audio from our content server. No information about you is sent with those downloads.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves your content and settings locally: journal entries and prompt answers, energy and mood check-ins, practice history, ratings and private notes, favourites, habits, rituals, affirmations, vision-board items, reminders, preferences you choose during onboarding (for example your needs and spiritual preference), and your subscription status. This data is encrypted on the device (AES-256) with a key kept in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). It is not sent to us or to anyone else. On iPhone, the encrypted data and its key are included in your device's own encrypted backups and phone-to-phone transfers (which you control in iOS settings), so your journal can move to a new iPhone; the key is never synced to iCloud Keychain. On Android, the key cannot leave the device, so use the App's journal export before changing phones.

3. Apple Health and Health Connect (optional)

If you choose to connect them, the App can read your recent sleep data to show you a pattern in Insights, and (on iPhone) write the time you spend practising to Apple Health as mindful minutes. Health data is processed only on your device. It is never sent to us, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with third parties. You can turn this off at any time in the App or in your device's health settings.

4. Guidance audio downloads

Some guidance audio (a natural, AI-generated voice) is downloaded from our content server, hosted on Cloudflare R2, the first time you play it. Premium members can also download it in the background on Wi-Fi. These requests contain only the name of the audio file. As with any internet request, the server necessarily receives technical information such as your IP address and device type in order to deliver the file. We do not attach any identifier to these requests, we do not combine them with any other data, and we do not keep access logs. Cloudflare's privacy policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play, which process your payment under their own privacy policies. To confirm that a subscription is active, renewed, cancelled or refunded, the App may use RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated ID (not linked to your name or email), the store receipt, the product purchased, its price and currency, and basic device and app information. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

6. Notifications

Reminders you turn on (for example a morning check-in, ritual or habit reminders) are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use push notification services and no notification content is sent through our servers.

7. Analytics

Usage statistics are off by default. If you switch them on in Settings → Privacy, the App records only coarse, non-sensitive events (never journal text, prompt answers or specific moods), and keeps them on your device, where you can view or clear them. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.

8. Sharing you choose

Some features let you share or export something yourself, for example exporting your journal as text, sharing a year-in-review card, or sending diagnostics when you contact us. These use your device's share sheet and go only where you send them. An exported journal is a plain-text copy outside the App's encryption, so only share it somewhere you trust.

9. What we do not collect

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, advertising identifiers or browsing history, and we do not sell or rent personal information.

10. Children

Kindlight is a general wellbeing app for adults and teenagers. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

11. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can erase everything at any time in Settings → Privacy & data → "Delete all your data", or by deleting the App. Downloaded audio can be removed in Settings → Appearance & sound. Because we do not hold your personal data on our own servers, there is nothing else for us to delete, but you may contact us with any request. Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat according to their policies.

12. Security

Your data is encrypted at rest on your device, and all network requests (audio downloads and purchase verification) use encrypted HTTPS connections.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because the App keeps your data on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the App. You can also contact us at any time.

14. Not a medical service

Kindlight supports everyday wellbeing. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis or in danger, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line; the App's Support resources list some options.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be reflected in an App update.
