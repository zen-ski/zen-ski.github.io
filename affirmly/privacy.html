PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile app "Affirmly: Daily Affirmations" (the "App"), published by Zenski ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it. The Android package ID is com.zenski.affirmation.

1. Summary

  • There is no account. We do not ask for your email address or phone number. A name you may give during setup stays on your device.
  • Everything you write or record in the App (journal entries, check-ins, intentions, your own affirmations, voice recordings, photos you add) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us.
  • We do not show ads, we do not track you across apps or websites, we use no analytics, and we do not sell any information.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • Nothing you write, say or record is sent to an AI service.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves your content and settings locally: your goals and tone preferences, saved and hidden affirmations, collections and playlists, mood check-ins, intentions, rituals, journal entries and any photos you attach, voice recordings, letters to your future self, journey progress, your garden, reminder settings and your subscription status. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Your phone's own backup (for example Google or iCloud backup), if you have it switched on, may include this data under that service's terms.

3. Camera, microphone and photos

The microphone is used only when you choose to record an affirmation in your own voice; the recording stays on your device. The camera is used only to show a live mirror view during the mirror practice; nothing is captured or saved. Photos you choose to attach to a journal entry are copied into the App's storage on your device. You can refuse any of these permissions and the rest of the App keeps working.

4. The natural reading voice

Affirmations can be read aloud in a natural voice that was recorded ahead of time with an AI text-to-speech service, and is labelled as AI-generated in the App. The App only plays these recordings; nothing is generated on your phone and nothing you write is sent anywhere. Recordings for free content come with the App. Others are downloaded from our content server, hosted on Cloudflare R2, the first time they play. These requests contain only the name of the audio file. As with any internet request, the server necessarily receives technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver the file. We attach no identifier, combine it with nothing, and keep no access logs. Cloudflare's privacy policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/. Your own affirmations are read by your phone's built-in speech engine.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Premium is bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store, which process your payment under their own privacy policies. To check whether a subscription is active, the App asks the store directly on your device. No purchase information is sent to us.

6. Notifications and widgets

Reminders you turn on are scheduled locally on your device. You can choose whether reminders show affirmation text or stay general. Home-screen widgets read from the App's storage on your device. We do not use push notification services.

7. Sharing you choose

You can share an affirmation image or export your data (You → Your data). These use your device's share sheet and go only where you send them.

8. What we do not collect

We do not collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers or browsing history, and we do not sell or rent personal information.

9. Children

Affirmly is a general wellbeing app for adults and teenagers. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can export or erase everything at any time in You → Your data, or by deleting the App. Because we hold no personal data on our servers, there is nothing else for us to delete, but you may contact us with any request. Purchase records are kept by Google and Apple under their policies.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because the App keeps your data on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the App. You can also contact us at any time.

12. Not a medical service

Affirmly supports everyday wellbeing. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis or in danger, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
