Abundly is a calm daily practice for your relationship with money: affirmations, guided visualizations, journaling and a personal 21-day path built around your Money Story.

Contact

Questions, ideas or something not working? Write to info.zenski@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

Subscriptions

Premium is a monthly or yearly subscription. Manage or cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, or on iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open Abundly → Settings → Restore purchases.

Your data

Abundly has no account and keeps everything on your phone, encrypted. To erase it all, open Settings → Privacy → Delete all my data. Read the privacy policy.

A gentle note

Abundly is for inspiration and self-reflection. It is not financial, medical or psychological advice, and results are not guaranteed.
