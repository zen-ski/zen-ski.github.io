PRIVACY POLICY

Updated November 25, 2026

Abundly is made by Zenski. This policy explains what the app keeps, where it keeps it, and the choices you have. The short version: your practice stays on your phone.

No account, no tracking

You can use Abundly without creating an account. We don't ask for your email, and we don't show ads.

Abundly has no analytics or advertising SDKs. We don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your device

Your Money Story answers, rituals, journal entries, scenes, vision boards, letters, intentions, check-ins and session history are stored only on your device.

The database is encrypted at rest. Its key is created on first launch and kept in your phone's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android). You can also lock the journal with Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or your device passcode.

Photos you add to scenes and vision boards, and voice recordings you make, are copied into the app's private storage and never leave your device unless you choose to share something.

Camera, microphone and photos

Mirror Work shows your camera preview so you can see yourself while you speak. Nothing is recorded or saved, and no image leaves your phone.

The microphone is used only when you record your own voice for a scene, a letter or an affirmation. Recordings stay on your device.

Photo access is used only for the pictures you pick. Abundly doesn't browse your library.

Voice, reminders and widgets

Personal narration is created by your device's built-in text-to-speech. The words are processed on your phone.

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Home-screen widgets read a small snapshot, such as today's affirmation and your Money Tree, from storage shared only between Abundly and its widgets.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

To check whether Premium is active, the app asks the App Store or Google Play directly on your device. No purchase information is sent to us or to any other service.

Sharing

When you share a card, an Abundance Check or a milestone, the image goes only where you send it, through your phone's share sheet.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time in Settings, under Delete all my data. This empties the database, removes your photos and recordings, cancels reminders and resets the app.

Deleting the app also removes its data from your device. Device backups you make, such as iCloud or Google backups, follow the settings of your phone.

Children

Abundly is made for adults and isn't directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. Important changes will also be shown in the app.

Questions about privacy? Write to us at info.zenski@gmail.com.
